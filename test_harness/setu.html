Frequently Asked Questions
How do I apply for MUDRA or PMEGP loan without collateral?

Under the CGTMSE credit guarantee framework, MUDRA loans up to ₹10 Lakhs and PMEGP projects up to ₹50 Lakhs are completely collateral-free. You only need your business plan, Aadhaar, and PAN.

What documents are needed for Udyam & Vishwakarma?

You need your Aadhaar Card (linked to active mobile number for e-KYC), PAN Card, and Bank Account passbook details with IFSC code. No registration fee is required.

How long does bank approval take after applying?

Under Public Sector Bank digital in-principle approval mechanisms, processing typically takes 7 to 14 working days once verified by your local branch or Sachivalayam.

Is there any fee to use Udyam Setu?

No, Udyam Setu is 100% free and open for all Indian entrepreneurs, farmers, students, and artisans.
